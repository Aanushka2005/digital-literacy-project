# Stay Safe Online Checklist

- Never share OTP with anyone
- Do not approve unknown payment requests
- Use strong and unique passwords
- Enable two-factor authentication
- Avoid public Wi-Fi for transactions
- Regularly check bank statements
- Install apps only from trusted sources
- Be cautious of unknown links

## UPI Safety
- Always verify the receiver before payment
- Never accept “collect request” from unknown users

## Report Cybercrime
- Website: https://cybercrime.gov.in
- Helpline: 1930
