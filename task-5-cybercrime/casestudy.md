# Cybercrime Case Study – UPI Fraud

UPI fraud is one of the most common cybercrimes in India. In this type of fraud, scammers trick users into sharing sensitive information such as OTPs or payment requests.

Typically, the fraud begins when a victim receives a call or message pretending to be from a bank or service provider. The scammer asks the user to approve a payment request or share an OTP. Once the user complies, money is instantly transferred from their account.

Students and individuals unfamiliar with digital payments are often targeted. The consequences include financial loss and stress.

This type of fraud highlights the importance of awareness and cautious behavior while using digital payment systems.
