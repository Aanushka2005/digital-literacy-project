Social Media Do’s and Don’ts

✅ Do’s
	•	Keep your profile neat and professional, especially if you’re using it for academic or career purposes
	•	Always use strong passwords and update them regularly
	•	Think carefully before posting anything online, as it can leave a permanent impression
	•	Be respectful and polite while interacting with others
	•	Double-check information before sharing to avoid spreading misinformation

⸻

❌ Don’ts
	•	Avoid sharing personal or sensitive information publicly
	•	Do not post anything offensive, abusive, or inappropriate
	•	Never spread fake news or unverified content
	•	Try not to get involved in unnecessary online arguments
	•	Do not click on unknown or suspicious links that could harm your data
