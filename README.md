Project Overview

This project was undertaken as part of the Digital Literacy course to develop a practical understanding of essential digital skills. The objective was to explore how digital tools can be used effectively for communication, collaboration, and building a professional online presence, while also understanding the importance of cyber safety in today’s digital environment.

Task Summary

Task 1 – Infographic
An infographic was designed using Canva to highlight key aspects of digital literacy. It covers fundamental concepts, safe internet practices, and the importance of maintaining a responsible and professional digital identity.
 Screenshot included in task-1-presentation folder.


Task 2 – Digital Portfolio
Profiles were created on platforms such as GitHub, LinkedIn, and HackerRank to establish a basic digital portfolio. This task helped in understanding how to showcase skills and maintain a professional presence online.
Screenshots included in task-2-portfolio folder.

Task 3 – Platforms
A beginner-level problem was solved on HackerRank to gain practical exposure. Additionally, a Google Form quiz was created to understand how online tools can be used for interaction and data collection.
Screenshots included in task-3-platforms folder.


Task 4 – Email Etiquette
Two professional emails were drafted to understand proper structure, tone, and clarity in communication. A checklist of social media do’s and don’ts was also prepared to promote responsible online behavior.
Files included in task-4-email-etiquette folder.


Task 5 – Cybercrime Awareness
A case study on UPI fraud was analyzed to understand common cyber threats. A preventive checklist was also created to highlight safety measures and promote awareness among users.
Files included in task-5-cybercrime folder.

 Conclusion

This project provided valuable insights into the effective use of digital tools and platforms. It also enhanced my understanding of professional communication and the importance of staying safe in the digital world, which are essential skills for every student today.
